Danh sách từ vựng

Lọc theo cấp độ CEFR

boarding pass

A2 noun

/ˈbɔː.dɪŋ ˌpɑːs/

Nghĩa tiếng Việt: thẻ lên máy bay
English: A document provided by an airline during check-in, giving a passenger permission to enter the aircraft.
“Please have your boarding pass and passport ready at gate 14.”
link Collocation: issue a boarding pass • digital boarding pass

layover

B1 noun

/ˈleɪˌoʊ.vər/

Nghĩa tiếng Việt: thời gian quá cảnh (giữa 2 chặng bay)
English: A short stay in a place that you make while on a longer journey to somewhere else.
“We had a four-hour layover in Doha before our flight to Frankfurt.”
link Collocation: brief layover • during the layover

baggage claim

A2 noun

/ˈbæɡ.ɪdʒ ˌkleɪm/

Nghĩa tiếng Việt: khu vực nhận hành lý ký gửi
English: The specific airport carousel area where arriving passengers retrieve their checked luggage.
“Follow the airport signs down to the baggage claim area on level 1.”
link Collocation: baggage claim carousel • proceed to baggage claim

itinerary

B1 noun

/aɪˈtɪn.ər.ər.i/

Nghĩa tiếng Việt: lịch trình, hành trình chi tiết
English: A planned route or detailed list of destinations and scheduled times for a journey.
“The travel agency emailed us a comprehensive itinerary for our weekend trip.”
link Collocation: travel itinerary • confirm the itinerary

departure gate

A1 noun

/dɪˈpɑː.tʃər ˌɡeɪt/

Nghĩa tiếng Việt: cửa khởi hành
English: An airport gate where passengers assemble to board their specific aircraft.
“Passengers on flight VN302 are invited to board at departure gate B22.”
link Collocation: head to departure gate • gate change announcement

customs declaration

B1 noun

/ˈkʌs.təmz ˌdek.ləˈreɪ.ʃən/

Nghĩa tiếng Việt: tờ khai hải quan
English: An official form that passengers fill in to list goods brought into a country upon entry.
“Remember to fill out your electronic customs declaration prior to landing.”
link Collocation: submit a customs declaration • customs inspection
