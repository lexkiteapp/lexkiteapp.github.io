pedagogy

NOUN C1
/ˈped.ə.ɡɒdʒ.i/

phương pháp giảng dạy, sư phạm học

The method and practice of teaching, especially as an academic subject.

“The school adopted an interactive pedagogy to foster student debate.”
link Collocations: progressive pedagogy • critical pedagogy

curriculum

NOUN B2
/kəˈrɪk.jə.ləm/

chương trình giảng dạy

The subjects comprising a course of study in a school or college.

“Modern AI literacy must be woven directly into the core curriculum.”
link Collocations: national curriculum • revise the curriculum

holistic

ADJ C1
/həʊˈlɪs.tɪk/

toàn diện, mang tính tổng thể

Characterized by the treatment of the whole person rather than just single symptoms.

ADJ B1
/kəmˈpʌl.sər.i/

Required by law or a rule; obligatory rather than elective.
