FRAME (identical on every Lexkite app screen): phone screen 390 px wide, one column, 16 px side margins, 8 px spacing grid, warm cream page. TOP BAR 56 px, one of four variants: HUB = Lexkite kite logo + 'Lexkite' wordmark on the left; on the right an 'IELTS' exam chip, a flame streak pill '14', a star XP pill '820', a round avatar 'L'. INNER = round tactile back-arrow button on the left, page title next to it, at most one round icon button on the right. MODAL = round close (X) button on the left, title, nothing on the right. FOCUS (lesson, test, game in play) = round close (X) button, a thick rounded progress bar, one small status chip on the right. BOTTOM TAB BAR 64 px on HUB and INNER screens only (never on MODAL or FOCUS): white bar with a top border, exactly 5 equal tabs, icon above label: Today (house), Vocab (open book, small red due badge), Skills (target), Tests (flag), Me (person); the active tab has a teal icon inside a soft teal pill and a bold teal label, the others dark grey ink. Cards: 16 px radius, 2 px border, 5 px bottom ledge. Primary button: full-width candy-orange tactile button with a 6 px darker ledge and deep brown-black label; on task screens it sits fixed at the bottom (above the tab bar if there is one), on hub screens it sits inline in its card. Empty or error state: one glossy illustrated icon, one short heading, one line of text, one primary button and one text button. No phone status bar, no device frame.Teacher slot (only where asked): an empty dashed rounded 120x140 box at the left directly under the top bar, its speech bubble to the right; never draw a person there.
