“Some people think children should start school at a very early age. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?”

Cambridge IELTS 18 · Formal Academic Essay with Balanced Evaluation & Clear Authorial Voice.

record_voice_over British RP (Dr. Vance) 02:18
tune Annotation Layers
§ 1 · Introduction Background & Thesis
52w

Whether young children should enter formal primary schooling at a very tender age remains a topic of spirited debate. While opponents argue that premature academic exposure can suppress innate creativity, I would contend that the developmental advantages lay the foundations for[đặt nền móng cho] lifelong learning, provided that curricula prioritise structured play over rote memorisation.

§ 2 · Body Paragraph 1 Cognitive & Social Gains
84w

On the one hand, enrolling infants in early educational settings significantly accelerates socialization. In a nursery environment, toddlers learn to negotiate peer relationships, share communal resources, and resolve minor conflicts under professional supervision. Moreover, a growing body of evidence[ngày càng nhiều bằng chứng] in cognitive neuroscience indicates that neuroplasticity peaks before age five. Exposure to structured linguistic and numerical stimuli during this window helps pupils develop advanced receptive vocabulary and numeracy habits that persist throughout their schooling.

§ 3 · Body Paragraph 2 Counter-argument & Nuance
87w

However, critics rightly caution that high-stakes instructional pressure can backfire. If toddlers are subjected to rigid desk work, they risk experiencing chronic anxiety and burnout before reaching adolescence. Far from accelerating progress[thay vì thúc đẩy, ngược lại], excessive parental expectations may erode a child’s intrinsic enthusiasm for discovery. Nonetheless, this pitfall stems from defective pedagogical design rather than early schooling per se. When institutions balance group activities with child-led play, such risks are largely mitigated.

§ 4 · Conclusion Restatement & Final Thought
62w

In conclusion, taking all factors into consideration[xét toàn diện mọi khía cạnh], the social and neural benefits of early institutional education comfortably eclipse the potential downsides. As a result, policymakers should invest in universal preschool programmes that nurture curiosity, safeguarding wellbeing while building robust academic foundations.

warning_amber Examiner Pitfalls to Avoid
expand_more
⚠️ Pitfall 1: Fence-sitting

Do not simply say "both sides have points". State clearly whether the advantages outweigh the disadvantages in the introduction and stick to it.

⚠️ Pitfall 2: Overusing mechanical linkers

Avoid starting every sentence with "Firstly, Secondly, Furthermore". Instead, use topic phrases and demonstrative pronouns for natural cohesion.
