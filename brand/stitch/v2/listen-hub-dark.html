Listening exam tips

  1. 1

    Read the questions first: Use the 30-second silent prep to predict keywords, word categories, and grammar forms.

  2. 2

    Beware of changed answers: Speakers often correct themselves (e.g. "Let's meet at 4... actually make it 4:30").

  3. 3

    Listen for paraphrasing: Recordings rarely reuse exact question words; listen for synonyms and antonyms.

  4. 4

    Strict word limits: Adhere to "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER" instructions to avoid penalty.

  5. 5

    10 minutes of dictation a day: Short daily sentence dictation drills sharpen your phonetic awareness and spelling speed.
