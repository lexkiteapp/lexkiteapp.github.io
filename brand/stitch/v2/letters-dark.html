Complaint about a noisy neighbour

Thư phàn nàn gửi hàng xóm về tiếng ồn đêm muộn (Căn hộ chung cư)

You live in an apartment and your neighbour plays loud music late at night. Write a letter to the neighbour. In your letter:

  • arrow_right Describe the problem and when the music occurs
  • arrow_right Explain how it affects you, your work, or your sleep
  • arrow_right Suggest a cooperative solution or mutually agreeable quiet hours
Band 8.5 Model Template 172 words

"Dear Mr Hall,

I am writing to politely bring to your attention an issue regarding noise levels from your apartment, unit 4B.

Over the past two weeks, loud music has been playing late into the night, particularly between 11:30 PM and 1:30 AM. Because our bedrooms share a common wall, the heavy bass vibrates through my room and makes it virtually impossible to fall asleep.

As a result, I have been arriving at work exhausted, which is severely impacting my focus during early morning meetings.

Could I kindly suggest that we observe the building’s designated quiet hours after 10:30 PM? Alternatively, using headphones during late hours would be greatly appreciated.

Yours sincerely,
David Miller"
