What we collect (and what we don't)

A clear checklist of data boundaries

Lexkite is engineered around the principle of minimal footprint. We only store the data strictly required to calculate your spaced-repetition memory intervals and reward your daily consistency streak.

check_circleWhat we collect
  • doneAccount Email & Handle: To keep your study streak synchronized.
  • doneStudy telemetry: Cards mastered, test scores, and streak days.
  • doneTarget Exam Date: Used only to calibrate your flashcard pacing algorithm.
cancelWhat we never touch
  • closePhone contacts & address books: Never requested or accessed.
  • closePrecise GPS coordinates: We never log physical location traces.
  • closeCredit card details: Processed via Stripe directly; we never store card numbers.
02

Audio & speech practice

How your voice recordings are handled

Lexkite includes tactile audio drills and IELTS pronunciation testing. We treat human speech as sensitive personal biometrics.

mic_off

Ephemeral Speech Processing

Your microphone input is converted directly into phoneme vectors in transient memory. Raw audio files are discarded within 10 seconds of pronunciation scoring unless you hit 'Save to Local Notebook'.

We never use learner voice samples to train general public generative models without explicit opt-in consent.

03

Cloud sync & storage

Local-first architecture that puts you in control

Every Lexkite vocabulary card, custom tag, and review timestamp is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) first. You can train offline completely uninterrupted.

04

Your rights & choices

Full portable ownership of your learning assets

You put hours into building vocabulary decks. You own that intellectual effort. At any moment in your account settings:

downloadUniversal Deck Export

Download all your saved cards, phonetic notations, and personal example sentences as clean JSON, CSV, or formatted Anki package (.apkg).

deletePermanent Purge

Trigger our automatic scrubbing worker. All database entries, study scores, and backups are purged irrevocably within 48 hours.

05

Children & learner safety

Strict compliance for schools and students under 16

Lexkite is compliant with GDPR-K and COPPA regulations. We do not permit public user profiles for minor accounts, nor do we run behavioural tracking or social discovery mechanisms that expose younger students to strangers.

school

Are you an ESL educator or school administrator? We provide custom FERPA-compliant Data Protection Agreements (DPAs) upon request.
