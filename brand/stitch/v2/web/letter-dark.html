IELTS General Task 1 — Band 8.5 Formal Letter Sample

Examiner-annotated model answer for IELTS General Training Writing Task 1 with vocabulary highlights, structure analysis, and scoring rubric.

OFFICIAL TASK PROMPT Cambridge General Training benchmark

You recently bought a piece of electronic equipment from a store, but when you got home, you found it was faulty.

Write a letter to the store manager. In your letter:

  • explain what you bought and when
  • describe the problem with the equipment
  • state what action you would like the store to take.
description

Model Answer (Band 8.5)

Annotated by Senior IELTS Examiner
182 words
Color Key: Polite opening & closing Clear purpose Cohesive links Formal vocabulary & collocations

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with a recent purchase made at your downtown branch on October 24th.

Specifically, I purchased an Apex Pro Wireless Headset (Receipt #AX-98421). Upon unpacking and testing the device at home, I discovered that the left ear cup produces an intrusive buzzing noise whenever active noise cancellation is engaged, and the battery fails to hold a charge beyond fifteen minutes.

Given that this item is brand new and covered by your standard 12-month manufacturer warranty, I would appreciate it if you could either arrange a direct replacement or issue a full refund to my original payment card. Enclosed with this letter are copies of the purchase receipt and guarantee slip.

Thank you in advance for your prompt attention to this matter. I look forward to your response.

Yours faithfully,

Alex Morgan

verified Uses 'Yours faithfully' because the manager's name was not specified in the prompt. Examiner Tip: Exactly 3 paragraphs for 3 bullet points.

Useful Phrases in this Letter (Band 8.0+)

Click audio to hear native British pronunciation
Stating Purpose

"I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with..."

Standard polite, formal opening for complaint letters without emotional exaggeration.

Chronological Sequencing

"Upon unpacking and testing the device..."

Preposition + gerund construction that shows high grammatical flexibility.

Logical Justification

"Given that this item is covered by..."

Connective participle phrase introducing firm legal or contractual justification.

Polite Formal Request

"I would appreciate it if you could either..."

Indirect conditional structure avoiding aggressive imperative demands.

Attachment Notice

"Enclosed with this letter are..."

Formal inversion structure common in official UK business correspondence.

Complimentary Close

"Yours faithfully"

Mandatory closing when starting with 'Dear Sir or Madam' (unnamed recipient).
