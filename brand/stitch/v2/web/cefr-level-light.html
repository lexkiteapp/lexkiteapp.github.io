Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to reach B1 from A2?
expand_more
Most learners require roughly 150 to 200 guided study hours. With Lexkite’s 5-minute daily micro-drills and spaced repetition, consistent practice helps learners bridge the A2–B1 gap in 8 to 12 weeks.
What IELTS score corresponds to B1?
expand_more
A CEFR B1 level aligns with an IELTS Overall Band Score of 4.0 to 5.0. It qualifies learners for standard student visas, vocational training, and everyday workplace communication.
Is B1 enough for university admission?
expand_more
Undergraduate and postgraduate degrees typically mandate B2 (IELTS 5.5–6.5) or C1 (IELTS 7.0+). However, foundation pathway years and pre-sessional language programs accept B1.
