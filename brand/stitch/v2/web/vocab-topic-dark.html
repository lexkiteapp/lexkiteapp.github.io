Adjective • C1

biodegradable

/ˌbaɪ.oʊ.dɪˈɡreɪ.də.bəl/

Capable of being decomposed by bacteria or biological living organisms.

"Governments should mandate biodegradable packaging to protect coastal marine sanctuaries."

Noun • C1

depletion

/dɪˈpliː.ʃən/

A reduction in the number, quantity, or available volume of something valuable.

"Rapid resource depletion threatens future food production across agricultural basins."

Verb • C1

deteriorate

/dɪˈtɪr.i.ə.reɪt/

To become progressively worse in quality, condition, or health over time.

"Air quality will steadily deteriorate unless emissions from heavy industry are taxed."

Adjective • B2

ecological

/ˌiː.kəˈlɑː.dʒɪ.kəl/

Relating to the relationships between living organisms and their environment.

"Deforestation leads to permanent ecological damage that accelerates species extinction."

Verb • B2

contaminate

/kənˈtæm.ə.neɪt/

To make something impure, poisonous, or unfit for use by adding toxic matter.

"Toxic agricultural runoff can contaminate freshwater reservoirs supplying major towns."

Adjective • B2

renewable

/rɪˈnuː.ə.bəl/

Naturally replenished and sustainable; not depleted when consumed.

"Switching subsidies toward renewable energy generation drives long-term economic gains."
