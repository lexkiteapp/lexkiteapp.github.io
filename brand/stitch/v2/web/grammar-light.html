Present Perfect

Connecting past actions to the present moment

stars Level B1–B2 timer 4 min read verified IELTS 7.0+ Core

1 When to Use It

3 Main Triggers
public

Life Experience

Things you have done anytime up to now. Exact date doesn't matter.

“She has visited Paris twice.” check_circle Keywords: ever, never, already
bolt

Present Result

Action finished recently, but its result matters right now in the present.

“I have lost my keys (can't enter).” check_circle Keywords: just, already, yet
all_inclusive

Unfinished State

Started in the past and still true right now today.

“They have known each other for 10 yrs.” check_circle Keywords: since 2018, for 5 years
warning

Top Exam Trap: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

-0.5 Band Slip

Never use present perfect with finished specific time markers (yesterday, last year, in 2021, when I was 10).

close

“I have seen him yesterday.”

Mistake: 'Yesterday' is a finished time
Incorrect
check

“I saw him yesterday.” or “I have seen him today.”

Correct: Specific past = Past Simple; Open day = Present Perfect
Accurate
lightbulb Quick Rule: If there is a clock date/calendar tag, drop "have". Keep it simple!
