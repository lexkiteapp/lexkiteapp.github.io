Our Commitment in Brief

  • check_circle Zero-Sponsor Influence: No sponsored vocabulary items, exam prep partners, or pay-to-feature pedagogical lists.
  • check_circle 100% Corpus Verification: Lexical distributions calibrated against standard IELTS academic papers and ETS TOEIC question pools.
  • check_circle Examiner-Led Human Audits: Every interactive sample sentence undergoes secondary human peer-review for natural register and phonetics.

How We Validate Vocabulary & Exam Prompts

At Lexkite, vocabulary acquisition is treated like tactile architecture. Language learners preparing for high-stakes assessments (IELTS Academic, General Training, and TOEIC Listening/Reading) cannot afford to memorize obscure archaic terms or uncalibrated idiomatic slips.

Our pedagogy bridges academic corpora with contemporary spoken discourse. Every unit item in Lexkite must clear a strict 4-stage empirical verification hurdle before reaching your interactive drill queue.

The Four Pillars of Lexkite Quality

1
check_circle

Corpus-Backed Frequency (COCA & BNC)

We cross-reference terms against the Corpus of Contemporary American English (COCA) and British National Corpus. Only items ranking in Band 6.5–8.5 academic utility are highlighted in core drills.

2
check_circle

Double-Blind Examiner Review (Band 8.5+)

Model answers and context dialogues are scrutinized by verified native-speaking certified IELTS trainers to eliminate artificial phrasing and grammatical over-complexity.

3
check_circle

Real Candidate Error Indexing

Our distractors (multiple-choice answer variants) simulate genuine candidate collocations and false-friend pitfalls, training instant situational discrimination under real exam clock pressure.

4
check_circle

Zero Algorithmic Hallucination Guarantee

Generative AI tools are strictly limited to drafting aid. No automated text enters drill sets without verified human sign-off, preventing synthetic lexical inaccuracies and awkward syntax.
