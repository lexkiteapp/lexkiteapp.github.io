The Future of Eco-Centric Megacities

Modern urban planning increasingly recognizes that rapid expansion requires ecologically resilient solutions. Historically, capital infrastructure focused primarily on concrete density and transport arteries.

Supporters argue that this can reduce pollution and help cities become more sustainable in the face of accelerated climate fluctuations.

By integrating green corridors and bio-climatic architectural envelopes, metropolitan zones can reduce municipal heat loads by up to 3.8 degrees Celsius while creating interconnected wildlife reserves.
