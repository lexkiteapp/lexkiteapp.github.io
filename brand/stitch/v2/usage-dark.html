Study tip: In 85% of exam errors, learners use "depend of" directly translated from French, Spanish, or Vietnamese. In English, it is always depend ON.

depend /dɪˈpend/

Regular verb • B1 Intermediate • High-Frequency Exam Core

To be determined or conditioned by something else; or to trust and rely on someone for support.

1

Mandatory Preposition

Rule #1
depend ON / UPON
Never "of"

Use upon in formal academic essays; use on in everyday speaking and writing.

format_quote

“Our weekend barbecue plans depend on the weather.”

Colloquial: "It all depends on who you ask."

Verb Patterns

3 Core Frames

Pattern A: depend on + noun / pronoun

“The success of this program depends on active student participation.”

Pattern B: depend on + -ing (gerund)

“Securing the scholarship depends on submitting the essay early.”

Pattern C: depend on + wh- clause (whether / how / what)

“The cost depends on how many nights you decide to stay.”

Easily Confused Pairs

Nuance Guide
depend on vs rely on Near Synonyms

Both take on. Rely emphasizes practical need or emotional confidence ("I rely on my assistant"), whereas depend often describes conditional causation ("Prices depend on supply").

depend vs trust Different Grammar

Trust takes no preposition ("I trust him"). Never say "I trust on him".
