Complete the sentence

⚡ Present Perfect

“She has lived here __________ 2019.”

Hint

TEACHER
Teacher AMA • Coach

“Think: a point or a length?”

!

Use since with a starting point in time, and for with a length of time.

Since + Point
  • since 2019
  • since 8:00 AM
  • since Monday
For + Length
  • for 5 years
  • for two hours
  • for a long time
Example: “She has lived here since 2019.”
