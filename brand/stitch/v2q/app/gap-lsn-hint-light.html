“Think: a specific point or a length?”

Look at the time expression right after the blank to choose the correct preposition.

Use since with a starting point in time, and for with an amount of duration.

SINCE + POINT
  • schedule since 2019
  • schedule since 8:00 AM
  • schedule since Monday
FOR + LENGTH
  • hourglass_empty for 5 years
  • hourglass_empty for two hours
  • hourglass_empty for a long time
EXAMPLE APPLICATION TARGET SENTENCE

“She has lived here since 2019.”

check_circle

2019 is a specific calendar starting year, not a total count of elapsed years.
