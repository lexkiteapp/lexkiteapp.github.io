“Think: a specific point or a length?”

Look at the time expression right after the blank to choose the right preposition.

Use since with a starting point in time, and for with a length of time.

SINCE + POINT

  • schedule since 2019
  • schedule since 8:00 AM
  • schedule since Monday

FOR + LENGTH

  • timelapse for 5 years
  • timelapse for two hours
  • timelapse for a long time
EXAMPLE TARGET QUESTION

“She has lived here since 2019.”

check_circle 2019 is a specific calendar year (starting point), not an amount of time elapsed.
