“Think: a point or a length?”

info

Use since with a starting point in time, and for with a length of time.

• SINCE + POINT
  • › since 2019
  • › since 8:00 AM
  • › since Monday
• FOR + LENGTH
  • › for 5 years
  • › for two hours
  • › for a long time
EXAMPLE: “She has lived here since 2019.”
