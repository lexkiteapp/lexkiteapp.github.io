“I am very interesting in this job because it give me many chance.”

Target exam: IELTS Academic (Task 2)

Improved Version

“I am very interested in this job because it gives me many opportunities.”

Why These Changes Work

1
interesting → interested (Participle Adjective)

Use -ed for people's personal feelings; -ing describes the thing causing the feeling.

2
give → gives (Subject-Verb Agreement)

Third-person singular pronoun “it” takes a singular verb ending in -s.

3
chance → opportunities (Lexical Precision)

“Chance” usually means luck/probability. In professional contexts, “opportunities” is the correct countable academic term.
