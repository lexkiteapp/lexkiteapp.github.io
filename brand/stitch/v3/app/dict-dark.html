resilient

C1 • Advanced Band 7.5+
UK /rɪˈzɪl.i.ənt/
US /rəˈzɪl.jənt/
school TEACHER-SLOT Task 2 Priority

“High-yield in IELTS Writing Task 2 when describing economies or climate adaptations!”

format_quote Core Meanings

1

Able to quickly return to a previous good condition after damage or illness.

Nghĩa: Có khả năng phục hồi nhanh chóng, kiên cường.
2

Able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions.

Nghĩa: Bền bỉ, chịu đựng tốt trước nghịch cảnh.
