Ama Tutor

“Think: a specific point or a length?”

since Refers to a specific starting point in time:
since 2019 since Monday since 8:00 AM
for Refers to a total length or duration of time:
for five years for two hours for a long time
check_circle
Application: Because 2019 is a specific year (a fixed starting point, not a quantity of time), since is the correct answer.
