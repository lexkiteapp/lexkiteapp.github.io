close
Agreement Error
are increasing
check
Singular Agreement Standard
is increasing
lightbulb
Key IELTS Distinction: ‘The number of...’ acts as a singular collective subject requiring a singular verb (is / was). In contrast, ‘A number of...’ acts as a quantifier meaning several / many and takes a plural verb (are / were).
