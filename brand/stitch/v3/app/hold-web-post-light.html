Article Key Takeaways

The Academic Word List (AWL) compiled by Averil Coxhead contains 570 headwords divided into 10 sublists. Mastering these word families covers over 10% of vocabulary in academic texts, significantly boosting your comprehension in IELTS Reading and range in Academic Writing Task 2.

Target level: B2 to C1 Academic Module

What is the Academic Word List?

The Academic Word List represents common vocabulary found across secondary and university textbooks in various disciplines including economics, humanities, medicine, and engineering.

Instead of testing obscure medical or engineering jargon, IELTS Academic examiners look for your mastery of transferable formal vocabulary—words such as analyze, derive, hypothesis, and significant.

How to Learn Word Families Effectively

Always learn all related grammatical forms at once rather than isolated lemmas. For example, when studying derive (verb), record derivation (noun), derivative (adjective), and common collocations such as derive from.

Academic Collocation Example Task 2 Ready

“Substantial evidence indicates that regional economic policies contribute directly to long-term community prosperity.”
