Describe a time when the weather changed your plans

Structured 3-part speech template with model phrases, transitions, and natural responses.

“Set the atmosphere early with vivid sensory words before diving into how you reorganized your afternoon!”

1

Part 1 • Warm-up Questions

2-3 mins

Q1: Does the weather affect your mood often?

Sample response: “Undoubtedly. Overcast drizzle dampens my motivation, whereas crisp sunny mornings give me a sudden surge of productivity.”
overcast drizzle surge of productivity

Q2: Do you check weather forecasts before heading out?

Sample response: “It has become second nature. I always skim hourly radar updates to steer clear of sudden downpours during my commute.”
second nature steer clear of
2

Part 2 • Long Turn Cue Card

1-2 mins

You should say:

  • Where you intended to go and what you planned
  • What weather occurred and how it manifested
  • What alternatives you chose instead
  • And explain how you felt about the entire outcome
Structured Model Story Cohesion Model

“Last spring, my colleagues and I had arranged an extensive outdoor barbecue in the national park. Everything was prepared down to the smallest detail until a torrential deluge rolled in without prior warning.”

“Instead of calling off the gathering entirely, we pivoted on the fly and relocated to an indoor board game bistro. Although our initial itinerary was disrupted, the intimate venue actually helped us bond much closer.”

Key Phrases & Collocations
torrential deluge pivot on the fly ruined beyond salvage silver lining
3

Part 3 • Discussion Topics

4-5 mins

Q: How does extreme weather impact modern urban infrastructure?

“Metropolitan transport hubs often face cascading delays. When drainage capacity fails under flash floods, urban logistics grind to an abrupt standstill.”

Q: Has technological forecasting diminished the surprise factor of weather?

“To a large extent, yes. Satellite modeling offers actionable foresight, enabling event organizers to formulate realistic contingency plans in advance.”
