What do you say when you miss the bus?

Natural phrases native speakers actually use

Daily English
Primary Expression
“I missed my bus.”
/mɪst/
info Plain meaning: The bus left before I got there.
autorenew Polite / Professional Alternative For boss or school

“I got held up and missed the bus.”

Explains you were delayed by circumstances, sounding respectful and responsible.

warning

Don't say: “I lost my bus.”
Say “I missed” for transport schedules.
