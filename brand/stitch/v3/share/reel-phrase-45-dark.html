What do you say when you miss the bus?

Natural everyday spoken phrases for English learners.

Key Spoken Phrase Common / Casual

“I missed my bus.”

/aɪ mɪst maɪ bʌs/
info
Plain meaning: The bus departed before I arrived at the bus stop, so I could not take it.
Polite / Message to Boss Formal

“I missed the bus, so I may be a few minutes delayed.”

Ama [teacher-slot]
Ama Exam Mentor

“Say ‘missed the bus’, never ‘lost the bus’. In English, you lose objects, but you miss transport!”
