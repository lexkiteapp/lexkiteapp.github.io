Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers regarding access, algorithms, and exam alignment.

Is Lexkite really 100% free?
expand_more
Yes. There are no paywalls, hidden credit card forms, or "hearts" lockouts that stop your practice. All core drills, spaced repetition decks, and the browser extension remain entirely open to all learners worldwide.
How does the spaced repetition algorithm (FSRS) work?
expand_more
Lexkite utilizes the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS), an advanced cognitive memory model that outperforms traditional SM-2. It measures word complexity and your response lag to compute the optimal moment to review words just as neural recall begins to fade.
Can I use Lexkite for TOEIC and TOEFL as well as IELTS?
expand_more
Yes. You can switch your exam focus anytime in your preferences. TOEIC prioritizes commercial collocations and speed reading, TOEFL focuses on academic synthesis and lecture terminology, and IELTS targets nuanced task rubrics.
Does the Chrome extension track my browsing activity?
expand_more
Never. The add-on only executes when you intentionally double-click a word or trigger the lookup shortcut. No site histories, session cookies, or personal data are collected or transferred.
Does it work on mobile phones?
expand_more
Yes! Lexkite runs smoothly in any mobile web browser and supports full offline drills when added to your home screen as a progressive web app.
