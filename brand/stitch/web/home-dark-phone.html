Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about learning with Lexkite.

Is Lexkite really free? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes, 100% free forever. There are no paid tiers, no locked premium lessons, no subscription walls, and no pay-to-refill hearts. Our entire curriculum and browser add-on are free for every learner.
Do I need an account? keyboard_arrow_down
You can start practicing immediately as a guest. Creating a free account takes 10 seconds and lets you sync your progress across mobile, desktop, and the Chrome add-on.
Which exams can I practice? keyboard_arrow_down
We specialize in IELTS (Academic and General Training), TOEFL iBT, and TOEIC Listening & Reading, with dedicated vocabulary lists and mock test modules for each.
Does the add-on work on every site? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes! The Lexkite Chrome Extension works seamlessly across news articles, academic journals, social media, and PDFs opened in your browser.
Which languages is the interface in? keyboard_arrow_down
Lexkite is translated into 15 languages, including Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Russian, French, and German, so grammar explanations stay clear.
