serendipity

/ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ə.ti/
DEFINITION [CEFR C1]

The occurrence of finding valuable or pleasant things by good luck or happy chance.

ACADEMIC CONTEXT & EXAMPLE

“Many scientific breakthroughs, including the accidental discovery of penicillin by Fleming, were products of pure serendipity.”
COMMON COLLOCATIONS:
pure serendipity a stroke of serendipity happy serendipity
