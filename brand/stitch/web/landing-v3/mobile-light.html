Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about learning with Lexkite.

Is Lexkite really free? expand_more
Yes, 100% free forever. There are no paid tiers, no locked premium lessons, no subscription walls, and no pay-to-refill hearts. Our entire curriculum and browser add-on are free for every learner.
Do I need an account? expand_more
You can start practicing instantly as a guest. Creating a free account lets you sync your spaced repetition memory deck across devices and save words from the Chrome add-on.
Which exams can I practice? expand_more
Lexkite specializes in academic and business exams including IELTS Academic & General Training, TOEFL iBT, and TOEIC Listening & Reading, plus general CEFR A1–C2 preparation.
Does the add-on work on every site? expand_more
Yes, the Chrome extension works on all standard web pages, news articles, PDFs, and reading materials without injecting ads or tracking your browsing activity.
Which languages is the interface in? expand_more
The interface and grammar rule explanations support 15 native languages including Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Russian, and Arabic.
