Q131 · Part 5 · 48 s
The executive board voted to _______ the deadline for the quarterly financial report in order to accommodate the auditor's recommendations.
(A) extensive
(B) extension ✕ Your answer
(C) extend ✓ Correct
(D) extensively
check_circle Why C is correct

The phrasal construction "voted to + [base verb]" requires an infinitive complement. "Extend" functions as the transitive verb head accepting "the deadline" as its direct grammatical object.

cancel Why the others are wrong
  • A: extensive is an adjective and cannot serve as the verb head.
  • B: extension is a noun; it cannot follow infinitive 'to' here.
  • D: extensively is an adverb qualifying an action, not the verb head.
