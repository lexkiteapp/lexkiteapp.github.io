The Lexkite Blog shares practical, evidence-based ways to learn English vocabulary and prepare for IELTS, TOEIC and TOEFL. Every article connects to something you can do right away in the free [Lexkite app](/) – no credit card needed.

## Who we write for

Self-learners who want a clear plan, busy people with 15–30 minutes a day, and students who need a certificate to graduate, study abroad or get a job. We prefer concrete advice, our own example sentences and steps you can follow today over generic tips.

## How every article is made

1. **Search intent:** what learners actually ask, and which answer helps them improve.
2. **Outline:** answer the question directly first, then go deeper.
3. **Draft:** 100% original writing; every English example sentence is our own.
4. **Fact-check:** test formats, score scales and timings are checked against the official test owners ([IELTS](https://ielts.org), [ETS – TOEIC](https://www.ets.org/toeic.html), [ETS – TOEFL](https://www.ets.org/toefl.html)). If we are not sure, we leave it out.
5. **Duplicate check:** no copying from other pages, including our own.
6. **Quality review:** each article is scored against a 100-point editorial rubric and published only at 90 or above.

The fact-check date is recorded for every article. When a test format changes, we update the article and show the update date at the top.

## Images and video
