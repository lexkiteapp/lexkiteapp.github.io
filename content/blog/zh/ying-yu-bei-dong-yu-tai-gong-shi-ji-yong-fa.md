在英语语法进阶过程中，熟练掌握 **英语被动语态**（Passive Voice）是写出地道、专业学术论文与商务报告的分水岭。在日常口语对话中，人们更倾向于使用主动语态（Active Voice）以突出说话者的动作；但在科技文献、官方公文、医学诊断以及严肃新闻报道中，被动语态被高频使用，用以营造客观、中立、不带个人偏见的专业语调。

## 英语被动语态的核心逻辑与基本公式

被动语态的核心本质在于**焦点的转移**：将叙述重心由“动作的执行者”（Agent）转移到“动作的承受者”（Receiver）或“动作造成的结果”上。

母语者在以下特定情境下会明确优先选用被动语态：
1. **执行者未知或无从查考：** *The ancient tomb was excavated in the late 19th century.*（无法准确指认具体是谁最先发掘的。）
2. **执行者不言自明，提及纯属冗余：** *The reckless motorist was apprehended immediately.*（逮捕罪犯显然是警察所为，无需赘述 by police。）
3. **动作承受者与事件结果具有压倒性重要性：** *Over 20,000 electric vehicles were assembled in July alone.*（关注点在于装配完成的车辆规模，而非流水线装配工个人。）
4. **外交与商务沟通中的委婉避责：** *An oversight was made regarding the contract clause.*（比直接指责 *You made a mistake* 更加职业与得体。）

## 被动语态万能公式：Be + 过去分词 (V3)

被动语态的结构形式永远由两个核心部分构成：
> **主语 + 助动词 be（随具体时态和人称变化） + 及物动词的过去分词 (Past Participle - V3)**

若需点明执行者，则在句末通过 **by + 执行者** 呈现。

### 常见8大时态被动语态推导对照表

| 时态类型 | 主动语态形态 | 被动语态公式 | 被动经典例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **一般现在时** | do / does | am / is / are + V3 | *The system logs are scrutinized daily.* |
| **现在进行时** | am/is/are doing | am/is/are + being + V3 | *The software architecture is being upgraded.* |
| **一般过去时** | did | was / were + V3 | *The treaty was signed in Geneva.* |
| **过去进行时** | was/were doing | was/were + being + V3 | *The laboratory samples were being analyzed.* |
| **现在完成时** | have / has done | have/has + been + V3 | *All quarterly milestones have been achieved.* |
| **过去完成时** | had done | had + been + V3 | *The patent had been granted prior to the merger.* |
| **一般将来时** | will do | will + be + V3 | *The comprehensive findings will be released soon.* |
| **情态动词** | modal + do | modal + be + V3 | *Ethical protocols must be strictly observed.* |

## 主动句改写为被动句的标准三步法

将一个主动句转换为标准的被动语态，遵循以下三步逻辑：

**原主动句：**
*The research team (主语) developed (谓语) a revolutionary energy storage unit (宾语).*

1. **提取宾语作为被动句新主语：** *A revolutionary energy storage unit...*
2. **确认原句时态并匹配 be 动词形式：** 原句为一般过去时，新主语为单数，因此 be 动词取 *was*，后接过去分词 *developed*；
3. **根据信息重要性决定是否保留执行者：** *by the research team*。

**转换后被动句：**
*A revolutionary energy storage unit was developed by the research team.*

## 绝不可用于被动语态的及物与不及物动词

这是英语考试中极其高频的易错点：**不及物动词（Intransitive Verbs）由于天然不接直接宾语，因此从逻辑上绝不可能产生被动语态**。

严禁使用被动语态的常见动词清单：
- *happen / occur / take place（发生）：* 绝不能写出 *The collision was happened*（错！），必须写 *The collision occurred*；
- *die（去世、死亡）：* 严禁出现 *The patient was died*（错！），应为 *The patient passed away / died*；
- *arrive（到达）：* 严禁 *The parcel was arrived*（错！），应为 *The parcel arrived*；
- *disappear / vanish（消失）：* 严禁 *The evidence was disappeared*（错！），应为 *The evidence disappeared*；
- *belong to（属于）：* 静态动词，只能用 *This property belongs to...*。

## 双宾语动词的被动语态转换（Direct & Indirect Objects）

当遇到能接“间接宾语（人）”和“直接宾语（物）”的双宾语动词（如 *give, send, award, offer, grant, lend, teach*）时，被动句有两种变法：

- 主动句：*The foundation awarded Dr. Vance a prestigious scholarship.*
1. **以人（间接宾语）为主语（英语国家日常使用最自然）：**
   *Dr. Vance was awarded a prestigious scholarship by the foundation.*
2. **以物（直接宾语）为主语（需补充介词 to 或 for）：**
   *A prestigious scholarship was awarded to Dr. Vance by the foundation.*

## 使役被动结构：Have / Get Something Done

当我们要表达“让别人帮我们完成某事”而非亲力亲为时，会采用使役被动结构：
- **公式：** *主语 + have / get + 物品 + 过去分词 (V3)*
- *The enterprise had its financial balance sheet audited by an external firm.*（企业聘请外部机构审计了财务资产负债表。）
- *We need to get our proprietary code reviewed before deployment.*（我们需要在部署前请专业人员完成专属代码审查。）

## 短语动词的被动语态处理原则 (Passive Voice with Phrasal Verbs)

在将包含动词短语（动词+介词或副词，如 *call off, look into, carry out, put up with, take care of*）的主动句转换为被动语态时，中国学生最常犯的错误是将短语原有的介词或副词遗漏。规则是：**短语动词作为一个不可分割的整体，原短语中的介词必须紧跟在过去分词之后，绝不能随意丢弃**：

- **主动：** *The oversight committee called off the emergency audit.*
- **被动：** *The emergency audit was called off by the oversight committee.*（off 必须完整保留）
- **主动：** *The forensic cyber team is looking into the suspicious server logs.*
- **被动：** *The suspicious server logs are being looked into by the forensic cyber team.*（into 必须紧跟 looked）
- **主动：** *They should carry out the safety inspections quarterly.*
- **被动：** *The safety inspections should be carried out quarterly.*

## 形式主语与客观被动句型 (Impersonal Passive): It is said that...

在严谨的科技论文、法律文书与主流严肃媒体报道中，当引用普遍观点、专家共识或未证实的公众传闻时，使用客观被动句（Impersonal Passive）可以有效剥离主观色彩：

### 结构一：It + 被动动词 (said / reported / acknowledged / estimated) + that 从句
- *It is widely acknowledged that continuous language exposure accelerates cognitive neural adaptation.*（学术界普遍认为，持续的语言暴露能够加速大脑认知神经的适应。）
- *It was reported that consumer confidence rebounded substantially in the final fiscal quarter.*（据报道，消费者信心在第四季度强劲反弹。）

### 结构二：真正主语 + 被动动词 + 动词不定式 (to do / to have done)
- *The proprietary pharmaceutical compound is believed to inhibit viral replication effectively.*（该专利化合物被认为能够有效抑制病毒复制。）
- *Dr. Mitchell is reported to have secured unanimous board approval for the expansion.*（据报道，米切尔博士的扩张计划已获得董事会全票通过。）

## 真实案例对比剖析 (Case Study): 企业年度报告中的语态转换

观察以下某跨国金融科技企业内部通告中的两版行文对比，感受语态对职业质感的决定性影响：

- **主动语态版本（显得局促、主观且充斥过多无实质价值的代词）：**
  *We launched the new algorithmic trading platform last Monday. Over two million active users downloaded our software update. However, our technical developers detected several synchronization anomalies. We are resolving these technical glitches immediately.*
- **被动语态版本（庄重、严谨、客观，聚焦点完全落在业务成果与技术解决上）：**
  *The next-generation algorithmic trading platform was officially deployed on Monday. More than two million software installations were registered within forty-eight hours of rollout. Although minor synchronization anomalies were initially identified during peak volume periods, comprehensive corrective patches have been engineered and deployed across all regional servers.*

通过语态的恰当切换，整个文案展现出了无可挑剔的企业专业度与严谨公信力。

## 常见问题 (FAQ)

### 1. 什么时候必须省略 "by + 执行者"？
当执行者泛指一般大众（如 people, someone, anyone）、由于常识显而易见、或者为了掩盖执行者身份时，应当主动省略 by 短语，以避免句式臃肿。例如：*English is spoken across the globe.* 无需写 *by people*。

### 2. 为什么进行完成时（如 has been being done）极少使用被动语态？
因为连续出现 *been being* 在语音上拗口重叠，现代英语约定俗成倾向于用简单的完成时被动（*has been done*）或直接改用主动句来传达。

### 3. "Get-Passive"（Get+过去分词）与标准 Be动词被动语态有什么区别？
Get-Passive（如 *get injured, get caught, get promoted*）通常局限于口语非正式表达，且往往带有“意外发生”、“突发性”或对主语产生实质影响的感情色彩；正式书面与学术写作中只应采用 *be + V3*。
