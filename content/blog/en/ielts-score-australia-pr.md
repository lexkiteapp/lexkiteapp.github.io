If you are searching for the **IELTS score for Australia PR**, the first thing to know is that Australia does not name one magic number. It names three English levels, **Competent, Proficient and Superior**, and each level is an IELTS band that you must reach in *every one* of the four skills: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. Higher levels can add points to a points-tested skilled visa, and some visas only ask for a minimum level. Below is what the Department of Home Affairs publishes, how to read it, and how to turn it into a study plan.

## The IELTS score for Australia PR: three English levels

Home Affairs describes English ability in levels, and each level page has a table of approved tests. For IELTS, Academic and General Training both appear, and the numbers are the same. We read the pages on **17 November 2026**:

| Level | IELTS (Academic or General Training), each of the 4 skills | Points in the Skilled Independent (subclass 189) points table |
|---|---|---|
| Competent English | at least 6 | 0 |
| Proficient English | at least 7 | 10 |
| Superior English | at least 8 | 20 |

*Sources: Home Affairs, English language pages for Competent, Proficient and Superior English; points table for the Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189), immi.homeaffairs.gov.au. Checked 17 November 2026.*

{{diagram: d1 | The three levels side by side: the band is the same in every skill, and only the higher levels add points}}

Three things stand out.

**The rule is per skill, not overall.** The pages say "in each of the 4 test components". A result of 8.0 overall with a 6.5 in Writing does not show Superior English, because Writing is below 8. Your overall band does not matter for this table, only your four skill bands. If you want to understand how the overall band is built, read our guide to [IELTS band scores explained](/en/blog/ielts-band-scores-explained/).

**Competent adds no points.** A 6 in every skill reaches Competent English, which some visas ask for as a minimum. It is a requirement, not a bonus. Proficient and Superior are where English becomes a lever.

**The points depend on the visa.** The figures above come from the points table of one visa, the Skilled Independent visa. Other visas may use different rules, include different points, or ask only for a fixed level. Always open the eligibility page of the exact visa you plan to apply for.

## What the 6, 7 and 8 mean in practice

A band is a statement about a test result, so the useful question is what it takes to produce it. These are practical study observations, not official descriptions.

**Reaching 6 in every skill** is mostly about removing the weak spot. Many candidates have one strong skill and one that holds them back. Typical blockers are running out of time in Reading, losing the thread in Listening Sections 3 and 4, a Writing answer that does not fully address the task, and Speaking answers that stop after one sentence. If you keep landing at 5.5 in one skill, our article on [how to break an IELTS band plateau](/en/blog/how-to-break-ielts-band-plateau/) is a good place to start.

**Reaching 7 in every skill** is a different job. Accuracy starts to matter as much as range: fewer slips in grammar, more precise vocabulary, and answers that develop an idea instead of listing it. Listening at 7 cannot afford many small losses such as spelling, plurals and number formats, so [IELTS Listening tips](/en/blog/ielts-listening-tips/) are worth a careful read. In Writing, the most common loss is not grammar but task response, so check the [common IELTS Writing mistakes](/en/blog/ielts-writing-mistakes/).

**Reaching 8 in every skill** is demanding and takes sustained, targeted work. Candidates who get there usually have an unusually even profile, with no skill below the others. If your four skills are far apart, a plan for Superior English should start by lifting the lowest one, not by polishing the highest.

## Which IELTS test should you book?

Home Affairs accepts both IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training, for tests taken on or after 7 August 2025. The scores in the table do not differ between the two. So the choice is practical: pick the version you can prepare for best, then make sure it is the one your visa accepts. If you are unsure which suits an immigration plan, read [IELTS General vs Academic for immigration](/en/blog/ielts-general-vs-academic-immigration/).

Several rules from the same pages deserve a note before you pay for a test.

- **Secure test centre only.** The department says tests must take place in a secure testing centre. It lists IELTS Online among the tests it does not accept, together with other at-home formats.
- **Three years.** For the levels above, the result must be one you achieved in the 3 years before your visa application. Tests taken on or before 6 August 2025 can still be used until 6 August 2028, depending on the visa.
- **One Skill Retake.** The department accepts IELTS results that include One Skill Retake for eligible visas. Whether your visa is eligible is on that visa's page, so do not assume it.
- **Other tests exist.** The Home Affairs English page also lists PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, Cambridge C1 Advanced, OET, CELPIP General, LANGUAGECERT Academic and MET, each with its own scores. If you are comparing, our [PTE vs IELTS for Australia](/en/blog/pte-vs-ielts-australia/) article lays out the trade-offs honestly.

Passport holders from Canada, New Zealand, the Republic of Ireland, the United Kingdom and the United States can also show Competent English by citizenship, with a valid passport from that country. That applies to Competent only; it does not replace a test if you want Proficient or Superior points.

## Why one weak skill decides the level

Because every skill needs the same band, the **lowest skill is your level**. This changes how you should spend your study hours.

Here is a made-up example. A candidate scores Listening 8.0, Reading 7.5, Writing 6.5 and Speaking 7.0. Reading the table: Listening alone would meet Superior; Reading and Speaking meet Proficient; Writing at 6.5 only meets Competent. The profile shows **Competent**, not Proficient, until Writing reaches 7. The candidate does not need more Listening practice. They need Writing to move one step.

That is why a plan for this goal begins with an honest picture of all four skills, not a favourite one. Gains in your weakest skill usually move your level more than extra work in your strongest.

## Before you book: a short checklist

- Open the English page for the level you want and read the table for IELTS.
- Open the eligibility page of your visa and check whether it asks for a level or a fixed score.
- Confirm the test is at a secure test centre and not an online format.
- Count three years from your test date to your planned application date.
- Keep a copy of your Test Report Form for the application.

Rules and charts change. If any detail here differs from the live page on the day you read it, the live page wins.

## Frequently asked questions

### What IELTS score do I need for Australia PR?

There is no single score. Australian pages define Competent (6 in each skill), Proficient (7) and Superior (8). Which one you need, or how many points you want, depends on the visa and on your points plan, so read the eligibility page for your visa.

### Do Academic and General Training both count?

Yes. For tests taken on or after 7 August 2025, the department lists both IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training, with the same band for each level.

### How long is my IELTS result valid for an Australian application?

For the levels in this article, the result must be one you achieved in the 3 years before your visa application, depending on the visa subclass. Tests taken on or before 6 August 2025 can be used until 6 August 2028, depending on the visa.

### Can I take IELTS at home?

No. The department says tests must be taken at a secure testing centre and lists IELTS Online among the formats it does not accept.

### Can I predict my official band from practice results?

No. Practice tools, including ours, can show progress and weak areas, but only the real test produces an official band. Use practice scores to decide what to study next, not as a promise.
