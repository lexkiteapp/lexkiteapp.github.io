Standard IELTS does not work for UK visas that need a Secure English Language Test (SELT); if you choose IELTS, you need IELTS for UKVI, which is one of several approved SELTs. Skilled Worker needs CEFR B2 (5.5 in each IELTS for UKVI component), and for a Student visa the level depends on your course.

This article explains what IELTS for UKVI is, which other tests GOV.UK approves, which routes need a SELT and what to check before you book.

## What is IELTS for UKVI and why does it exist?

**IELTS for UKVI** is a government-approved Secure English Language Test (SELT). Under UK immigration law, applicants who must demonstrate English language ability to secure a visa, work permit, or settlement status must complete their test at a testing location certified by the Home Office ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt)).

The fundamental distinction between standard IELTS and IELTS for UKVI is **security and certification**, not difficulty. GOV.UK lists which routes need a SELT, and for IELTS the approved products are IELTS for UKVI and IELTS for UKVI Life Skills.

{{diagram: d1 | Technical and operational comparison between standard IELTS and IELTS for UKVI.}}

### What is identical
- The test format, structure, and timing.
- The question papers (Listening, Reading, Writing).
- The face-to-face Speaking interview format.
- The scoring rubrics and band descriptors (Band 0 to 9.0).

### What is different
- **Security:** UKVI tests have an extra security layer and are taken only at centres approved for them.
- **In person only:** IELTS for UKVI is taken in person at an official centre.
- **Test locations:** Not every IELTS test centre is approved for UKVI, so confirm that your centre and date are for the UKVI version when you book.
- **Fees:** IELTS for UKVI typically costs more than the standard test; check the booking page for the current price.

## IELTS for UKVI is one approved test among several

GOV.UK's SELT page says it is for you to decide which approved test to take. IELTS for UKVI is one option; the list also includes:

- **LANGUAGECERT:** International ESOL SELT, Academic SELT and General SELT (the older four-skills ESOL SELT was replaced by the Academic and General versions from 6 July 2025).
- **Pearson:** PTE Academic UKVI or PTE Home.
- **PSI:** Skills for English UKVI.
- **Trinity College London:** SELT for UKVI (ISE or GESE).

Approved providers in the UK are Trinity College London, the IELTS SELT Consortium, LANGUAGECERT and Pearson. Outside the UK they are PSI Services (UK) Ltd, the IELTS SELT Consortium, LANGUAGECERT and Pearson. The rest of this article covers the IELTS route; if you are weighing PTE as well, our [PTE vs IELTS for Australia](/en/blog/pte-vs-ielts-australia/) comparison explains how that test works.

## Which UK visa routes ask for a SELT?

Your visa category dictates the level and whether you need all four skills or only speaking and listening. If you choose IELTS, the products are the four-skill IELTS for UKVI and the Life Skills variant:

### 1. Skilled Worker Visa
- **Level required:** **CEFR B2** in all four components (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking).
- **Equivalent IELTS for UKVI score:** at least **5.5** in each of the four components. The CEFR-to-IELTS mapping comes from [ielts.org's page on IELTS for UK visas](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-tests-for-uk-visas-and-immigration), so confirm it there before booking. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/knowledge-of-english) sets B2 as the standard level, and B1 applies only to people who already held the visa before 8 January 2026 and are extending it.
- **Exemptions:** Nationals of some countries are exempt from the English requirement; check the GOV.UK list for yours.
- **Test format:** GOV.UK's SELT page lists the approved IELTS products as IELTS for UKVI and IELTS for UKVI Life Skills, so if you choose IELTS for Skilled Worker, you book the four-skill IELTS for UKVI. GOV.UK's SELT page does not say which IELTS module applies, so confirm it on your booking page. For how Academic and General Training are treated in other countries, see [IELTS General vs Academic for immigration](/en/blog/ielts-general-vs-academic-immigration/).

### 2. Student visa
- **Below degree level:** the SELT you need is at CEFR B1, which is 4.0 in each component of IELTS for UKVI.
- **Degree level or above:** the requirement is CEFR B2, which is 5.5 in each component, and IELTS or IELTS for UKVI both work; the higher education provider may also assess your English itself.
- Your offer letter says which applies, so do not book until you have read it (check the Student visa English page for your course level).

### 3. Family, Spouse, and Citizenship Routes (Life Skills)
- Visas such as Spouse/Partner (A1 for initial entry, A2 for extension) and Indefinite Leave to Remain / Citizenship (B1) require only speaking and listening. For these routes, you sit the simplified **IELTS for UKVI Life Skills** test rather than the four-skill test. The level depends on the route, so confirm it on GOV.UK before you book.

## What B1 and B2 mean in IELTS for UKVI scores

The UK Home Office maps visa requirements to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Each component must reach the level on its own:

- **CEFR B1:** at least 4.0 in each component. It applies, for example, to Student visas below degree level, and to Skilled Worker applicants who already held the visa before 8 January 2026 and are extending it.
- **CEFR B2 (the standard for Skilled Worker):** at least 5.5 in each component.

A critical rule: if your visa demands CEFR B2 across all four components, scoring 8.0 in Reading and Speaking but 5.0 in Writing means you do not meet the language requirement. Balanced preparation across all skills matters. To see what strong written answers look like, browse the [IELTS Writing model answers hub](/en/samples/).

Writing is where one weak score is easiest to miss; [linking words for IELTS writing](/en/blog/linking-words-for-ielts-writing/) helps there.

## Test day checks and your result

Because a UKVI result is used as evidence in a visa application, GOV.UK sets conditions on the test itself:

- It must be on the approved list and sat at an approved location; for IELTS for UKVI that means in person at an official centre.
- If your route needs several components combined, they must all be achieved in the same combined test.
- In some cases you may resit one failed component; check your provider's terms and conditions before you count on it.
- Check your booking confirmation for the accepted ID and for your centre's instructions.

A steady routine before the test helps you stay focused on the day; our guide to [daily English habits for IELTS](/en/blog/daily-english-habits-for-ielts/) shows one way to build it.

## Which test and route fit you? A short checklist

- Does your route list a four-skill test or only speaking and listening? Only four-skill routes use IELTS for UKVI; the others use the Life Skills version.
- Does the GOV.UK SELT page name the test and the provider you plan to use? Remember that it is for you to decide which approved test to take.
- Is an approved location available in your country, and is your date inside your visa timeline?
- If you are a student, does your offer letter say below degree level (B1, 4.0 each) or degree level or above (B2, 5.5 each)?
- Will the result have been awarded within two years before you apply?

## A worked example: reading a report against the CEFR mapping

The report below is invented for illustration. Listening 6.5, Reading 6.5, Speaking 6.0, Writing 5.0.

- **Skilled Worker (B2, 5.5 in each component):** Writing 5.0 is below 5.5, so this report does not meet it, even though the other three scores are higher. The components are not averaged.
- **Student visa below degree level (B1, 4.0 each):** all four scores are 4.0 or higher, so the report would meet it.
- **Degree-level Student visa (B2, 5.5 each):** the same Writing score fails it.

The same report passes or fails depending only on the route, which is why you read the route first and the scores second.

## How long a UKVI result stays valid

GOV.UK requires a SELT to have been awarded within the two years before you apply, so check that your result is still inside that window on the day you apply. The same rule applies to the other approved tests, not only IELTS for UKVI.

## Common candidate errors to avoid when booking UKVI

1. **Booking standard IELTS to save money:** If your visa officer requires a SELT, a standard IELTS score—no matter how high—will not be accepted.
2. **Assuming the UKVI version is harder than standard IELTS:** The papers and scoring are the same; the difference is security and certification.
3. **Mixing up Life Skills and the four-skill test:** Spouse and settlement routes use IELTS for UKVI Life Skills (speaking and listening only), while Skilled Worker needs the four-skill test, so check which one your route names.
4. **Neglecting test centre travel times:** Check where the nearest approved UKVI centre is, and book a seat early enough to avoid rushing.
5. **Assuming one test fits every purpose:** a professional regulator or university may set its own English requirement, so check theirs as well as the visa's.

## Self-check: UKVI regulations

1. Is the Reading section harder in IELTS for UKVI than in standard IELTS?
2. Name two UK routes that need only speaking and listening.
3. Which provider names, besides the IELTS SELT Consortium, are approved to run SELTs in the UK?
4. Which acronym names the secure tests the Home Office accepts as proof of English?
5. A candidate scores Listening 6.5, Reading 6.5, Speaking 6.0 and Writing 5.0 on IELTS for UKVI. Does that report meet B2 for Skilled Worker?

**Answers**
1. **No.** The papers, difficulty and scoring are the same.
2. **Any two of:** citizenship, partner, parent, settlement (ILR) and some others on the GOV.UK SELT page.
3. **Trinity College London, LANGUAGECERT and Pearson.**
4. **SELT** (Secure English Language Test).
5. **No.** B2 needs at least 5.5 in each component, and Writing 5.0 is below it, however high the other scores are.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use standard IELTS for a UK Skilled Worker visa?

No. For routes that require a SELT, you need a test on the approved list, such as IELTS for UKVI, and a standard IELTS result is not accepted for those routes.

### Should I book travel before my IELTS for UKVI result arrives?

Check the release time on the booking page for your test type and centre (computer-delivered and paper tests differ). Do not pay the visa fee or book travel until you hold the result.

### How long is an IELTS for UKVI result valid?

Two years. GOV.UK treats a SELT result as valid for two years from the date it was awarded, so it must have been awarded in the two years before you apply.
