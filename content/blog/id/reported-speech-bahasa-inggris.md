**Reported speech bahasa Inggris** (kalimat tidak langsung) adalah cara menyampaikan ulang ucapan orang lain tanpa mengutip kata per kata. Rumus dasarnya: **subjek + said / told someone + (that) + kalimat yang tenses-nya dimundurkan satu langkah**. Contoh: *"I am busy," Dika said* → *Dika said (that) he **was** busy.* Ada empat hal yang berubah: **tenses** (*am → was, will → would*), **kata ganti** (*I → he*), **keterangan waktu dan tempat** (*tomorrow → the next day, here → there*), dan untuk kalimat tanya, **urutan kata** kembali seperti kalimat berita (*He asked where I lived*, bukan *where do I live*).

Bagi pelajar Indonesia, bagian tersulit justru perubahan tenses. Dalam bahasa Indonesia, kita cukup bilang "Dika bilang dia sibuk" tanpa mengubah apa pun. Dalam bahasa Inggris, kata kerja harus ikut "mundur" ke masa lalu. Materi ini juga sering keluar di soal UTBK dan tes masuk kerja, biasanya dalam bentuk mengubah kalimat langsung menjadi tidak langsung.

## Direct speech dan reported speech: apa bedanya

**Direct speech** mengutip ucapan persis, dengan tanda kutip:
*Sinta said, "I have finished my thesis."*

**Reported speech** melaporkan isi ucapan, tanpa tanda kutip:
*Sinta said that she had finished her thesis.*

Kata *that* boleh dipakai atau dihilangkan, terutama dalam percakapan: *Sinta said she had finished her thesis.* Kata kerja yang paling umum adalah *say* dan *tell*, tetapi ada banyak pilihan lain seperti *explain, mention, promise, suggest, admit*, yang akan dibahas di bawah.

## Rumus perubahan tenses dalam reported speech bahasa Inggris

{{diagram: d1 | Setiap tense mundur satu langkah ketika kata kerja pelapor dalam bentuk lampau}}

Aturan ini disebut **backshift**: kalau kata kerja pelapor dalam bentuk lampau (*said, told, asked*), tenses dalam ucapan biasanya mundur satu langkah ke masa lalu.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| *"I **live** in Bogor."* | *He said he **lived** in Bogor.* |
| *"I **am working** from home."* | *She said she **was working** from home.* |
| *"I **bought** a new laptop."* | *He said he **had bought** a new laptop.* |
| *"We **have booked** the tickets."* | *They said they **had booked** the tickets.* |
| *"I **will send** the file."* | *She said she **would send** the file.* |
| *"I **can** help you."* | *He said he **could** help me.* |
| *"You **must** submit it today."* | *The lecturer said we **had to** submit it that day.* |

*Past perfect, would, could, should, might* tidak berubah karena sudah "paling jauh".

**Kapan tenses tidak perlu dimundurkan?**

- Kalau kata kerja pelapor dalam bentuk present: *She says she **is** tired.* (Dia baru saja bilang, atau sering bilang.)
- Kalau isinya masih benar sampai sekarang: *My teacher said the Earth **goes** around the Sun.* Backshift juga boleh (*went*), tetapi tidak wajib.
- Dalam percakapan santai, jika kejadiannya baru saja terjadi: *He just told me he **is** coming.*

Untuk ujian dan tulisan formal, pakai backshift secara konsisten kecuali ada alasan jelas. Kalau kamu masih bingung membedakan *simple past* dan *present perfect*, baca dulu [perbedaan present perfect dan simple past](/id/blog/perbedaan-present-perfect-dan-simple-past/).

## Perubahan kata ganti, waktu dan tempat

Saat melaporkan ucapan, sudut pandangnya berubah. Karena itu kata ganti dan keterangan juga ikut menyesuaikan.

**Kata ganti:** *I → he/she, we → they, my → his/her, you → I/me/we* (tergantung siapa yang bicara dan kepada siapa).

**Keterangan waktu dan tempat:**

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| *now* | *then / at that time* |
| *today* | *that day* |
| *yesterday* | *the day before / the previous day* |
| *tomorrow* | *the next day / the following day* |
| *last week* | *the week before / the previous week* |
| *next month* | *the following month* |
| *ago* | *before / earlier* |
| *here* | *there* |
| *this / these* | *that / those* |

Contoh: *"I'll meet you here tomorrow," Bayu said.* → *Bayu said he would meet me there the next day.*

Perubahan ini logis, bukan hafalan buta. Kalau kamu melaporkan pada hari yang sama dan di tempat yang sama, *today* dan *here* tetap boleh dipakai.

## Say atau tell?

Ini kesalahan yang sangat sering muncul, termasuk di tulisan pelajar tingkat lanjut.

- **Say** + (that) + kalimat: *She said (that) she was late.* Kalau mau menyebut pendengarnya, pakai *to*: *She said to me that…*
- **Tell** + orang + (that) + kalimat: *She told **me** (that) she was late.* *Tell* hampir selalu butuh objek orang.

Jadi *She said me…* dan *She told that…* sama-sama salah.

Pengecualian dengan *tell* tanpa orang: *tell the truth, tell a lie, tell a story, tell the time*.

## Kalimat tanya dalam reported speech

{{diagram: d2 | Pertanyaan tidak langsung memakai urutan kalimat berita}}

Pertanyaan tidak langsung memakai kata kerja *ask, want to know, wonder*, dan **urutan katanya kembali seperti kalimat berita**: subjek dulu, lalu kata kerja. Tidak ada *do/does/did* dan tidak ada tanda tanya.

**Pertanyaan dengan kata tanya (wh-):**
*"Where is the library?"* → *She asked where the library **was**.*
*"What time does the bus leave?"* → *He asked what time the bus **left**.*

**Pertanyaan yes/no:** pakai *if* atau *whether*.
*"Are you coming?"* → *She asked **if** I was coming.*
*"Did you pay?"* → *He asked **whether** I had paid.*

Kesalahan khas: *He asked me where do I live* atau *She asked what is my name*. Kesalahan ini muncul karena dalam bahasa Indonesia urutan kata tanya tidak berubah. Biasakan membaca keras-keras pola yang benar: *asked where I lived, asked what my name was.*

## Perintah, permintaan, saran

Untuk perintah dan permintaan, gunakan **told/asked + orang + to + kata kerja**:

- *"Close the door."* → *He told me **to close** the door.*
- *"Please send me the report."* → *She asked me **to send** her the report.*
- *"Don't be late."* → *The manager told us **not to be** late.*

Untuk saran, pakai *suggest*: *"Let's take a break."* → *She **suggested taking** a break* atau *She suggested that we take a break.* Jangan tulis *suggested us to take*. Pola *suggest + -ing* berkaitan dengan materi [gerund dan to infinitive](/id/blog/gerund-dan-to-infinitive/).

## Kata kerja pelapor selain say dan tell

Supaya tulisan dan jawaban Speaking tidak monoton, kamu bisa memakai kata kerja pelapor yang lebih spesifik:

| Kata kerja | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| *explain* | *explain (to someone) that…* | *He explained that the server was down.* |
| *promise* | *promise to + V* | *She promised to call back.* |
| *admit* | *admit + -ing / that…* | *He admitted making a mistake.* |
| *deny* | *deny + -ing* | *She denied taking the money.* |
| *remind* | *remind someone to + V* | *He reminded me to bring my KTP.* |
| *warn* | *warn someone not to + V* | *They warned us not to swim there.* |
| *offer* | *offer to + V* | *My colleague offered to drive me home.* |
| *refuse* | *refuse to + V* | *The client refused to sign.* |

Kata kerja seperti ini sangat berguna di IELTS Writing Task 2 dan saat menulis laporan rapat. Banyak di antaranya juga muncul dalam kalimat pasif formal (*It is said that…*), lihat [passive voice bahasa Inggris](/id/blog/passive-voice-bahasa-inggris/).

## Kesalahan khas pelajar Indonesia

| ✗ Salah | ✓ Benar |
|---|---|
| *He said he is sick yesterday.* | *He said he was sick the day before.* |
| *She said me she was busy.* | *She told me she was busy.* |
| *He asked me where do I work.* | *He asked me where I worked.* |
| *She asked me am I ready.* | *She asked me if I was ready.* |
| *My boss told me don't be late.* | *My boss told me not to be late.* |
| *He said that he will come tomorrow.* (dilaporkan seminggu kemudian) | *He said that he would come the next day.* |

Semua kesalahan ini punya akar yang sama: bahasa Indonesia tidak mengubah bentuk kata kerja dan tidak mengubah urutan kata dalam pertanyaan tidak langsung. Jadi latihannya harus fokus pada dua hal itu.

## Latihan: ubah ke reported speech

1. *"I am studying for the exam,"* Fajar said.
2. *"We moved to Yogyakarta last year,"* they said.
3. *"Will you join the meeting?"* my manager asked me.
4. *"Where did you buy that bag?"* she asked me.
5. *"Don't forget your passport,"* Mum told me.
6. *"I can't come tomorrow,"* Lina said.
7. *"I have never been to Lombok,"* he said.

**Kunci jawaban:**

1. *Fajar said (that) he **was studying** for the exam.*
2. *They said (that) they **had moved** to Yogyakarta **the year before / the previous year**.*
3. *My manager asked me **if/whether I would join** the meeting.*
4. *She asked me **where I had bought** that bag.*
5. *Mum told me **not to forget** my passport.*
6. *Lina said (that) she **couldn't come the next day**.*
7. *He said (that) he **had never been** to Lombok.*

Kalau kamu salah di nomor 3 dan 4, ulangi bagian kalimat tanya: urutan subjek + kata kerja, tanpa *do/did*.

## Pertanyaan yang sering diajukan

### Apakah tenses selalu harus dimundurkan dalam reported speech?
Tidak selalu. Kalau kata kerja pelapor dalam bentuk present (*says*) atau isi ucapan masih benar sampai sekarang, tenses boleh tetap. Untuk soal ujian dan tulisan formal, backshift biasanya yang diharapkan.

### Apa bedanya said dan told?
*Said* tidak langsung diikuti orang (*she said that…*, atau *said to me*). *Told* selalu diikuti orang yang diajak bicara (*she told me that…*). *She said me* dan *she told that* sama-sama salah.

### Bagaimana mengubah kalimat tanya yes/no ke reported speech?
Gunakan *if* atau *whether*, lalu urutan kalimat berita tanpa tanda tanya: *"Are you hungry?"* → *She asked if I was hungry.*

### Apakah kata that wajib dipakai?
Tidak wajib setelah *say* dan *tell*. *He said that he was tired* dan *He said he was tired* sama-sama benar. Dalam tulisan formal, *that* sering dipakai agar kalimat lebih jelas.
