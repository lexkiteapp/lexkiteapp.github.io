Privacy Policy

Applies to the Lexkite web app (lexkite.com), the Lexkite iOS/Android apps and the “Lexkite” browser extension. Last updated: October 16, 2026.

Data controller: MODIAN LUXURY JEWELRY LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA (“Lexkite”, “we”). Contact: support@lexkite.com.

1. Summary

  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising.
  • Your learning data (saved words, context sentences, review progress) is stored on your device. It is copied to our servers only if you choose to sign in, so that it can sync between your devices.
  • The browser extension does not collect or send your browsing history or page content. Only the word or text you actively double-click, select or tap is sent to the lookup services listed below. Optional on-page features (highlighting words you have saved, underlining academic words) run entirely on your device.
  • Writing check (off by default). If you turn on “Writing check” in the extension, the text you type in a text box is checked for English mistakes on your device (Lexkite rules and, if your Chrome has it, Chrome’s built-in AI proofreader). Only if you also turn on “Advanced check” is the text of the box you are typing in sent to LanguageTool (api.languagetool.org) to find more mistakes; it is not sent to us. Password, email, number and payment-card fields, banking and payment sites, and sites you turn it off for are never checked. Mistakes you repeat are saved only on your device in your Mistake notebook (a short sentence excerpt), and words you often misspell may be added to your flashcards (the word only, without your text).

3. Third-party services

When you look up a word or translate a sentence, only that word or sentence is sent to the following services to get a result, without your identity or history:

  • Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com): meanings, definitions and sentence translation
  • Free Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev) and Wiktionary (Wikimedia): pronunciation and phonetic spelling
  • Openverse (api.openverse.org): Creative Commons and public-domain illustrations, always shown with author credit
  • Tatoeba (api.tatoeba.org): bilingual example sentences
  • Datamuse (api.datamuse.com): word suggestions and related words
  • MyMemory (api.mymemory.translated.net): backup translation
  • LanguageTool (api.languagetool.org): only when you press “Check grammar”, the text in the editor is sent to find grammar and spelling mistakes; in the extension, only if you turn on Writing check and Advanced check, the text of the box you are typing in is sent for the same purpose (at most 20 checks per minute)
  • Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com): only if you add your own Gemini API key and ask the extension to explain an expression with AI; the expression and its sentence are sent using your key. Your key is stored only on your device. Without a key, the extension may use the AI built into Chrome, which runs on your device.
  • YouTube: on youtube.com, the extension loads the subtitles of the video you are watching so you can tap words in them.

Our website and services are hosted by Cloudflare. Audio files are served from GitHub Pages and fonts from Google Fonts; like any website, these providers receive technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) needed to deliver the files.

4. Browser extension permissions

  • Access to websites (http/https): to show the lookup card when you double-click a word, translate text you select, look up words in YouTube subtitles and, if you turn these options on, highlight words you have saved, underline academic words on the page or check the English text you type in text boxes (Writing check). Page content is processed on your device and is never sent to us; only the word or text you choose is sent to the lookup services above.
  • storage: to keep your vocabulary and progress on your device.
  • alarms, notifications: daily study reminders and “golden time” review reminders.
  • contextMenus: the “Look up / Translate selection” item in the right-click menu.
  • scripting: to enable word lookup in tabs that were already open when you installed the extension, and to read the subtitle list of the YouTube video you are watching.
  • search: the search box on the Lexkite new tab page sends your query to your default search engine through Chrome.
  • New tab page: the extension shows a vocabulary card on new tabs; you can turn it off in the extension settings.

5. Your rights

  • You can export all your data (Settings → Export backup) or delete all data on your device at any time.
  • Sign out to stop syncing. To delete the data stored on our servers, email us from the address you signed in with; we delete it within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes the data stored in the extension.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data and to object to its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Data retention and security

Synced learning data is kept while your account is in use and deleted on request. Anonymous statistics and error logs are kept only as long as needed to improve the service. Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to our systems is limited to authorised administrators.

7. Children

Lexkite is intended for learners aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If we introduce advertising or another new type of data processing, we will update this policy before it starts.

9. Contact

For any question or request about your personal data (access, correction, deletion), contact support@lexkite.com or use the contact form. We reply within 30 days.
