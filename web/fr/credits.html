Image credits

Lexkite shows small pictures next to concrete words (for example apple, bus or umbrella) so they are easier to remember. All pictures are stored on Lexkite's own servers; the app never loads images from other websites. This page lists where the pictures come from and their licences.

Icons

Most word icons are from Noto Emoji by Google, licensed under the Apache License 2.0. When an icon is not available there, we use OpenMoji, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Icons are placed on a plain background and resized; the drawings are not otherwise changed.

Words are matched to icons using the English emoji names from the Unicode CLDR project, checked by hand.

Illustrations

Other word pictures are made by Lexkite and belong to Lexkite.

Photos from Wikimedia Commons

Some dictionary pages show a photo from Wikimedia Commons, found through Wikidata. We only use photos whose licence allows reuse (CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA). Photos are resized to fit a square; nothing else is changed. Each photo keeps its original licence. Select a file name to see the original, its author and the full licence.
