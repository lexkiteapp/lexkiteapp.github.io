Privacy Policy

Applies to the Lexkite web app (lexkite.com), the Lexkite iOS/Android apps and the “Lexkite” browser extension. Effective date: November 5, 2026. See also our Terms of Use.

This policy was prepared from a template and describes how Lexkite currently works. It is not legal advice and does not replace review by a qualified lawyer.

Data controller: MODIAN LUXURY JEWELRY LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA (“Lexkite”, “we”). Contact: support@lexkite.com or the contact form.

1. Summary

  • You can use Lexkite without an account. Your learning data (saved words, context sentences, review progress, settings) is then stored only on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google and allow it, your learning data syncs between your devices through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which our servers cannot read. Otherwise (or as a backup), a copy is stored on our servers. A basic account record (name, email address) is kept on our servers.
  • We measure how the product is used with our own statistics, without cookies. They use a random identifier stored on your device; if you sign in, this identifier is linked to your account (see section 2).
  • Text you submit for AI feedback is processed by Google Gemini or Cloudflare Workers AI.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Lexkite shows no advertising today; advertising is planned for the mobile apps and the website and will only be personalised with your consent (section 7).
  • The browser extension does not collect your browsing history or page content; only the word or text you actively choose is sent to the lookup services listed below.
  • You can export your data, delete it on your device and ask us to delete the server copy at any time: see How to delete your data.

3. Why we use your data (purposes and legal bases)

  • Providing the service (learning features, account, sync between devices, AI feedback you request, QR transfer, leaderboard you join, replying to your messages and reports): performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Usage statistics, error reports and improving the product: our legitimate interest in understanding how Lexkite is used and fixing problems (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time (section 9).
  • Security and abuse prevention (rate limits, spam checks, temporary blocks): our legitimate interest in keeping free features available to everyone (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Optional features you switch on (for example “Advanced check” in the extension, on-device speech recognition, joining the leaderboard) and personalised advertising when it is introduced: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligations (for example answering lawful requests from authorities): Art. 6(1)(c).

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. AI scores are practice feedback only.

5. Cookies and local storage

Lexkite does not use cookies for statistics, tracking or advertising. Instead, the app stores data in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB, in the app’s storage on your phone, or in the extension’s storage:

  • Strictly necessary / functional: your learning data and settings, interface language (including a country code detected once from our own server to choose the default language), sign-in session (stored by Firebase Authentication), offline copies of the app and audio files (service worker cache), and the state of features you use.
  • Language cookie: if you choose a language on our website, a first-party cookie named lk_lang remembers it for one year so that lexkite.com opens in that language. It holds only the language code and is not used for statistics, tracking or advertising.
  • Statistics: the random visitor identifier and sampling settings used for our own usage statistics (section 2).
  • Abuse prevention: a random device code sent with requests to AI feedback, Roleplay and QR transfer.

Third-party content may set its own cookies or storage: Cloudflare Turnstile when you open the contact form, YouTube when you play an embedded video and Google when you sign in. You can delete all Lexkite data stored in your browser at any time by clearing the site data for lexkite.com in your browser settings (this also deletes unsynced learning data).

When advertising is introduced, the ad providers will use cookies or the device advertising identifier; in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we will ask for your consent first through Google’s consent tool (section 7).

6. AI features

  • AI feedback on essays and speaking, and Roleplay replies, are generated by Google Gemini or Cloudflare Workers AI (Meta Llama models). Requests are sent from our server; your name and email are not included.
  • Free AI features have daily limits. To apply them, we count requests by account ID, random device code and hashed IP address.
  • AI output can be wrong. It is practice feedback only and is not an official score (see our Terms of Use).
  • In the app and the extension, you can optionally add your own Gemini API key; it is stored only on your device and requests are then sent directly from your device to Google using your key.

7. Advertising

Lexkite is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are not switched on yet: until they are, Lexkite loads no advertising code. When we switch them on, ads are shown by Google AdMob in the iOS and Android apps and by Google AdSense on the website, under these rules:

  • Where ads can appear: one clearly labelled “Advertisement” space on the app’s home screen, at the end of blog articles (plus one in the middle of long articles), below the main entry on dictionary pages and, on the website, below the results card after you finish a practice round (not on your first day and not when you scored under 50%); in the mobile apps, an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a study session (at most once every 3 sessions and once every 10 minutes, never on your first day); and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for extra AI turns, to save your streak, for a “second chance” to redo the questions you got wrong, or to double the XP of a round. Rewarded videos are limited per day, and after you watch one you see no full-screen ads for 30 minutes. If no video is available, the second chance is offered free once a day.
  • Where ads never appear: during lessons, quizzes, tests, reviews, speaking or role-play practice; in Classroom mode for teachers and students; for users we know are under 13; in the browser extension; and in the admin area.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): ads are requested only after you answer the consent message from Google’s certified consent management platform (IAB TCF v2.2). If you refuse, no ads are requested; if you accept only some purposes, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. In the apps the same choice is collected through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Settings › Privacy & ad choices in the app or on the website.
  • US state privacy laws (including the CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell your personal information for money. Personalised ads may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out through the “Do not sell or share my personal information” choice in Google’s consent message, and we treat a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal as an opt-out (non-personalised ads with restricted data processing).
  • What Google receives: to show, limit and measure ads, Google may use cookies or the device advertising identifier, your IP address and device and app information (how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services). On iOS we ask for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.
  • What Lexkite measures: daily counts only – ad requests, ads shown, clicks, rewards and the consent outcome by broad region (EEA/UK/Switzerland, USA, rest of world, derived from the country of the request; we do not store your IP address). To check that ads do not harm learning, a random 10% of devices see no ads, and we compare next-day and 7-day return rates and session length between the two groups; if the group with ads does worse, ads are automatically shown less often.
  • Rewarded ads: to credit extra turns, Google sends our server a signed confirmation containing a random device code (and your account ID if you are signed in), the reward and a transaction ID. We use it only to add the turns.
  • Google advertising cookies: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to Lexkite or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to Lexkite and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google My Ad Center (or Ads Settings), and opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu. On your phone you can reset or limit the advertising identifier in the device settings.

9. Your rights and how to delete your data

Export: Settings → Export backup downloads all your learning data as a file.

Delete data on your device: Settings → Delete all study data deletes your flashcards, progress, XP and statistics on that device. You can also clear the site data for lexkite.com in your browser, uninstall the mobile app, or remove the extension (this deletes the data stored in it).

Delete your account and server data: in the web app or the mobile app, open Settings → account card → Delete account (or go to lexkite.com/app/#/settings?delete=account), sign in again to confirm, and your synced learning data, account record, the link between your visitor identifier and your account, your leaderboard data and your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication are deleted immediately; problem reports you sent are kept without your email or account ID. You can also email support@lexkite.com with the subject “Delete my Lexkite data” from the email address you sign in with, or use the contact form with that address. We will delete your synced learning data, account record, the link between your visitor identifier and your account, your leaderboard data, your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication and, on request, your problem reports and messages, within 30 days, and confirm by email. Your Google account itself is not affected. Anonymous statistics that are not linked to your account may be kept for the periods in section 8.

Stop syncing: sign out at any time; your data then stays only on your device. You can also remove Lexkite’s access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam’s personal data protection law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing (including our usage statistics), to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. Contact us to exercise them; we reply within 30 days and may need to verify your identity. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children, students and schools

Lexkite is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In the EEA and the UK, users under 16 (or the lower age set by their country) should sign in only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. We never show ads to users we know are under 13 or to Classroom accounts, and we do not show personalised ads to users we know are under the applicable age of digital consent.

Classroom mode. Teachers can create a class and invite students with a join code or a Google Classroom share link. We collect only what the class needs: the student’s chosen display name (we recommend a first name and class, not a full name), assignment results and last activity time. We do not show the student’s email address or photo to the teacher, we do not request any Google Classroom or Google Workspace permissions, and we do not sell student data or use it for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than providing Lexkite to the school.

No ads for classroom accounts. Once you create or join a class, Lexkite does not show advertising on that device, and the browser extension never shows ads.

Students under 13 (COPPA). If a school lets students under 13 use Lexkite, the school is responsible for obtaining any consent required under the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and may consent on behalf of parents only for the school’s educational purposes. We use such students’ data solely to provide the service to the school. Parents may review or request deletion of their child’s data through the school or by contacting us.

US schools (FERPA). When a US school uses Lexkite, students’ class results may be education records under the school’s control. We act as a “school official” with a legitimate educational interest, use those records only to provide the service, do not disclose them to third parties except our service providers listed in section 4, and delete them at the school’s request. Teachers can remove a student or delete a class at any time; students can leave a class at any time; deleting an account deletes all its classroom data.

Guide for school IT admins: lexkite.com/schools.

11. International transfers

Lexkite is operated from the United States and uses providers (Cloudflare, Google, GitHub and others) that process data in the United States and other countries. Cloudflare serves requests from data centres around the world. Where data from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland is transferred, we rely on the safeguards offered by these providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

12. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can read and write only its own synced data, verified with Google-signed sign-in tokens. IP addresses used for abuse prevention are stored only as one-way hashes. QR transfers are encrypted on your device. Access to our systems is limited to authorised administrators, and administrative actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

13. Browser extension

  • The extension does not collect or send your browsing history or page content. Only the word or text you actively double-click, select or tap is sent to the lookup services in section 4. Optional on-page features (highlighting words you have saved, underlining academic words) run entirely on your device.
  • Writing check (off by default). If you turn on “Writing check”, the text you type in a text box is checked for English mistakes on your device (Lexkite rules and, if your Chrome has it, Chrome’s built-in AI proofreader). Only if you also turn on “Advanced check” is the text of the box you are typing in sent to LanguageTool to find more mistakes (at most 20 checks per minute); it is not sent to us. Password, email, number and payment-card fields, banking and payment sites, and sites you turn it off for are never checked. Mistakes you repeat are saved only on your device in your Mistake notebook (a short sentence excerpt), and words you often misspell may be added to your flashcards (the word only).
  • Deep explanation (AI). Only when you tap “🤖 Deep explanation” in the translation card, the English text you selected and the sentence around it are explained by Chrome’s built-in AI on your device if it is already available. Otherwise they are sent to our server (lexkite.com/api/ai) with a random device identifier used only for the daily free limit, and processed by Google Gemini or Cloudflare Workers AI as described in section 2. If you added your own Gemini key, it is used only when our free AI cannot answer. Nothing is sent until you tap the button.
  • Once a day the extension sends us the usage counts described in section 2. These counts never include the words you look up, page addresses, your name or email. When you uninstall the extension, a page on lexkite.com opens where you can optionally tell us why.
  • Permissions: access to websites (http/https) to show the lookup card, translate selected text, look up words in YouTube subtitles and run the optional on-page features above; storage to keep your vocabulary and progress on your device; alarms and notifications for study reminders; contextMenus for the right-click “Look up / Translate selection” item; scripting to enable lookup in tabs that were already open when you installed the extension and to read the subtitle list of the YouTube video you are watching; search so the search box on the Lexkite new tab page sends your query to your default search engine. The new tab page can be turned off in the extension settings.
  • Limited Use: Lexkite’s use of information received through the extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use this data only to provide and improve the extension’s single purpose (learning English vocabulary while you browse); we do not sell it, do not use or transfer it for advertising or to determine creditworthiness, and do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security or legal reasons, or in aggregated form. Information received from Google sign-in and Google Drive is used only to sign you in and sync your own learning data, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: we do not transfer it to others, use it for advertising, or let humans read it except with your consent, for security or legal reasons.
